# Louise — réceptionniste

Personnage fictif généré le 18 octobre 2026 avec l'outil intégré `image_gen` (fond transparent). Aucun portrait de personne réelle n'a été utilisé. L'image WebP conserve l'alpha de la génération et pèse environ 80 Ko. Elle sert dans le décor et dans le portrait du dialogue.

Fichier : `louise-receptionniste-v1.webp` (1024 × 1536).

Le personnage est un détourage photographique orienté vers le visiteur, pas un humain 3D volumétrique. Les objets et le comptoir l'occultent normalement ; le test des clics respecte la silhouette transparente. Le gros plan affiche la photographie complète.

## Prompt final

```text

Asset type: transparent full-body character cutout for a French A1 interactive 3D luxury hotel reception, fictional receptionist Louise.
Primary request: create one convincing photographic adult female palace hotel receptionist, around 35 years old, welcoming and professional, natural discreet smile, looking straight ahead at the arriving guest. Dark brown hair in a neat low bun, navy tailored blazer with matching knee-length skirt, ivory blouse, small elegant muted teal silk neck scarf, black closed-toe pumps. Arms relaxed in front, hands gently together at waist height, anatomically natural fingers. A small plain gold lapel pin, no readable badge text.
Composition/framing: full body head to shoes, upright, exactly front facing, eye-level camera, long lens with minimal perspective distortion; centered with only a small clear margin above head and below shoes. Arms close to torso. One person only.
Lighting: soft natural hotel interior daylight from front-left, realistic skin texture, subtle fabric weave, neutral white balance, no hard shadow, no rim glow.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no floor, no scenery, no counter, no objects, no cast shadow outside the figure. This cutout will be placed behind a 1.1 metre high marble counter, while preserving the complete body for close-up inspection.
Constraints: a natural real human photographic appearance, not an illustration, mannequin or 3D-rendered person; complete uncropped silhouette; no lettering, logos or watermarks.
```
